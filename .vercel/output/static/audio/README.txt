Bakuran original audio
======================

  lazy-afternoon-tambayan.mp3
      Original song by the game's author — "Lazy Afternoon Tambayan".
      This is the looping background music. Pause/play lives on the
      side of the screen.

The remaining files were synthesized for this game (Python additive
piano / pad / noise synthesis). They are original works, not ripped
from commercial recordings, and may be redistributed with the game.

  ambience-day.mp3      Original courtyard loop (day / Araw sa Bakuran)
  ambience-sunset.mp3   Original late-night piano+pad (sunset / Takipsilim)
  ambience-night.mp3    Original darker pad loop (night / Gabi-gabi)
  ambience-search.mp3   Original walking bed (Hanap-Buhay)
  ambience-rest.mp3     Original resolve bed (Tapos Na)
  foot-1.mp3            Packed-earth footstep variant A
  foot-2.mp3            Packed-earth footstep variant B
  foot-3.mp3            Packed-earth footstep variant C
  foot-4.mp3            Packed-earth footstep variant D
  pickup.mp3            Short bell chime on successful collect
